## 2. Strict Content Invariants

To respect intellectual property rights and maintain legal compliance, the following items are **strictly prohibited** from this repository:

### Prohibited Items (Do NOT Include):
- ❌ **Direct Copyrighted Book Text**: Verbatim excerpts, full chapters, or large passages copied directly from the Harry Potter novels or companion books.
- ❌ **Movie Scripts & Screenplays**: Verbatim dialogue transcripts or official screenplay excerpts.
- ❌ **Ripped Game / Media Assets**: 3D models, textures, audio clips, voice lines, sound effects, or animation files extracted from official video games or media.
- ❌ **Official Logos & Emblems**: Official trademarked movie logos, house crests, Warner Bros. shield logos, or official brand icons.
- ❌ **Official Artwork & Stills**: Promotional photos, film stills, official book cover art, or licensed illustrations.
- ❌ **Trademark-Imitative Branding**: Styling, badging, or trade dress intended to deceive users into believing Commonroom is an official product.
- ❌ **Large Franchise Scrapes**: Bulk copyrighted datasets scraped or exported from proprietary commercial platforms.

---

## 3. Permitted & Recommended Practices

All contributions, assets, test fixtures, and code within Commonroom must adhere to these standards:

### Permitted Standards:
- ✅ **Original UI & Artwork**: All iconography, UI themes, visual indicators, and illustrations must be original creations, open-source icon sets (e.g., Lucide, Material Icons, Feather), or licensed royalty-free assets.
- ✅ **Original Descriptions & Summaries**: Factual summaries of lore, creature properties, and spell effects written in original phrasing.
- ✅ **Structured Metadata & Taxonomies**: Original structured schemas (e.g., JSON schemas classifying spell types, potion ingredients, or house attributes) derived from factual reference.
- ✅ **Academic & Canonical Citations**: Fair-use factual references and citations (e.g., citing book titles and chapter numbers for quiz provenance) without reproducing substantial text.
- ✅ **Clear Fan-Project Positioning**: Prominently displaying the unofficial fan project disclaimer across all user-facing applications and documentation.

---

## 4. Guidelines for Coding Agents and Contributors

When authoring code, creating tests, or seeding databases:
1. **Never use copyrighted prose in test fixtures**: Use synthetic dummy data, generated phrases, or generic placeholders (e.g., `"Spell Name"`, `"A magical potion brewed under moonlight"`, `"Question 1"`).
2. **Never import proprietary datasets**: Do not commit JSON dumps or CSV files downloaded from unauthorized scraper repositories.
3. **Verify asset licensing**: Ensure any SVG, audio, or font asset added to the repository possesses an explicit, compatible open-source license (e.g., MIT, Apache 2.0, OFL).
4. **Sanitize LLM outputs**: Ensure prompts designed for AI agents emphasize summarization, structural analysis, and citation rather than verbatim reproduction of source texts.
